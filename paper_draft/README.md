Important novelty boundary:
The paper must not claim that speaker normalization, speaker-relative prosody, neutral baselines,
or enrollment-based personalization are new. Bone et al. (2012), Busso et al. (2013), and later
personalization work already establish these ingredients.

The distinctive contribution supported by the current experiments is the controlled connection
between **target between/within-speaker structure** and **representation reference-frame utility**.
