## Metrics and interpretation

Old Top10 count, lost count/fraction, unchanged/improved/worsened counts, signed rank delta P50/P95, conditional worsened loss P50/P95/max. Score drift standardized by before-query score std; fraction of oldTop10 answers with |drift|<=1e-3 and their loss fraction. This threshold is a descriptive bin, not an effect/significance gate. Absolute scores from different models are not numerically comparable. Near-constant positive score plus loss demonstrates lack of sufficient rank protection for those observations; does not establish sole mechanism or algorithm-wide 'score-only' objectives. Include new retrieval Hits10, SafeSucc where a defined edit/probe unit exists, pool size, update unit, model, probe split and information scope. Null SafeSucc means not defined for the large snapshot-learning unit, not zero/failure.
