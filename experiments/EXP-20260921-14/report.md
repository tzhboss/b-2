# Experiment Report — EXP-20260921-14

The target-reference mechanism generalizes beyond pitch level, loudness, and speaking rate.

Pitch variability, pause ratio, and voiced ratio each show significantly negative
Relative-minus-Absolute slopes as between-speaker Arousal/Dominance target structure increases.
The combined temporal/variability representation shows an especially strong effect.

This materially broadens the mechanism: it is not only about subtracting a speaker-specific mean
from pitch level, but also applies to stable speaker differences in expressive range and temporal
speech organization.

Decision: pass.
