# Experiment Report — EXP-20260921-15

Speaker-level inference strengthens the main MSP mechanism and narrows the correct interpretation of
IEMOCAP.

On MSP, all four Arousal/Dominance target-reference slopes remain strongly negative with
speaker-cluster 95% confidence intervals fully below zero.

On IEMOCAP, all four point slopes retain the negative direction but their speaker-cluster confidence
intervals cross zero because only ten speakers are available. The pure within-speaker endpoint
remains robustly positive.

The paper should therefore state:
- strong confirmatory evidence on MSP;
- directionally consistent but low-powered external evidence on IEMOCAP;
- robust within-speaker Relative advantage in both corpora.
