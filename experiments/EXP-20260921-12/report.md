# Experiment Report — EXP-20260921-12

## Main Result

The target-reference matching mechanism replicates across MSP and IEMOCAP using exactly the same
semantically aligned acoustic feature family: pitch plus speaking rate.

Increasing between-speaker target structure makes Relative progressively less favorable than
Absolute for Arousal and Dominance in both corpora under both linear and quadratic Ridge.

At the pure within-speaker target endpoint, Relative is better in all eight corpus-by-target-by-model
confirmatory cells.

## Why this experiment matters

IEMOCAP loudness provenance is ambiguous, so loudness is excluded from both corpora here.
The replication therefore does not depend on corpus-specific loudness definitions.

## Decision

pass.
