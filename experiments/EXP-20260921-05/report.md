# Experiment Report — EXP-20260921-05

## Main Result

Target-reference coupling generalizes across pitch, loudness, and speaking rate, but the effect
strength is strongly attribute- and corpus-dependent.

MSP is dominated by pitch and loudness, while rate carries a much smaller coupling. IEMOCAP shows
weaker slopes overall, consistent with its much smaller between-speaker target structure.

## Interpretation

Reference-frame matching is not a pitch-only phenomenon. The strength of the mechanism depends on
how strongly each acoustic attribute's stable speaker baseline covaries with the between-speaker
component of the target.
