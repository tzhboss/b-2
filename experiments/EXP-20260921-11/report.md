# Experiment Report — EXP-20260921-11

## Main Result

Content-disjoint speaker identification provides a more conservative trait-information result than
gender classification.

Relative prosody consistently lowers speaker-ID Macro-F1 compared with Absolute all-prosody, but
the reduction is modest. The preregistered strong-suppression criterion is not met.

Speaker baseline information nevertheless carries substantial identity structure, especially in
ESD and RAVDESS, and Relative+Baseline strongly increases speaker-ID decoding in all three corpora.

## Paper consequence

The paper should say that prosodic normalization can suppress stable speaker-trait information,
with gender as a strong example, while speaker identity itself is only modestly suppressed by these
three low-dimensional prosodic summaries.

## Decision

mixed.
