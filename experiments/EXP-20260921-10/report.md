# Experiment Report — EXP-20260921-10

## Main Result

After removing all ambiguous loudness fields, IEMOCAP still replicates the target-reference
matching mechanism using only pitch and speaking rate.

For Arousal and Dominance, increasing between-speaker target structure makes Relative
progressively less favorable than Absolute under both linear and quadratic Ridge.

## Interpretation

The external replication is not an artifact of the undocumented relative_db feature.
Pitch and speaking rate alone are sufficient to reproduce the mechanism.

IEMOCAP can therefore remain in the main paper as a semantically safe external validation, with
loudness excluded from its primary evidence.

## Decision

pass.
