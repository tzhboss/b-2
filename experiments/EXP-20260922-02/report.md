# Experiment Report — EXP-20260922-02

Moderate unlabeled enrollment recovers the practical Hybrid benefit of the full six-attribute interpretable prosody representation.

At K=20, K-shot Hybrid exceeds Absolute by +0.0390 CCC for Arousal and +0.0318 for Dominance. Increasing enrollment from K=20 to K=50 adds only +0.0093 Arousal CCC and +0.0074 Dominance CCC, satisfying the preregistered practical plateau criterion.

At K=50, the K-shot Hybrid is within 0.0044 CCC of the marginal-oracle Hybrid for Arousal and within 0.0037 for Dominance. Thus a finite unlabeled enrollment set recovers nearly all of the raw-target speaker-center benefit of the full interpretable representation.

All six center dimensions show monotonically decreasing estimation error as K increases. K=20 should be described as a practical moderate-enrollment operating point rather than a hard saturation boundary, because the Arousal K20-to-K50 confidence interval still extends above 0.01 even though the preregistered mean-gain criterion passes.

Decision: pass.
